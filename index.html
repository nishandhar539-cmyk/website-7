Yes. Every subscription runs on a real, actively maintained premium account. We never use cracked, hacked, or stolen logins — if an account ever has an issue, we replace it free of charge.
How fast will I get access after paying?
Most orders are delivered within 10 minutes during our active hours. If you order late at night, it may take a little longer, but you'll always get a WhatsApp confirmation once it's ready.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and bank transfer, along with international cards for select plans. Payment details are shared once you message us to confirm your order.

Absolutely. All plans are billed monthly with no lock-in contract. You can upgrade, downgrade, swap included apps, or cancel before your next renewal date at no extra cost.
What happens if my subscription stops working?
Message us on WhatsApp or Messenger and we'll fix or replace it, usually within the hour. Every plan includes this support at no additional charge for the length of your subscription.
